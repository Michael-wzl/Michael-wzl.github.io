I am a third-year undergraduate at the University of Hong Kong, majoring in Financial Technology, and an undergraduate research assistant at the [Trustworthy AI and Systems Research (TASR) Lab](https://github.com/HKU-TASR), advised by [Prof. Ka-Ho Chow](https://khchow.com).

My research focuses on protecting personal photos from face recognition and image retrieval systems. I work on both privacy protection methods and the tools needed to evaluate them. I am also interested in adversarial machine learning, responsible AI, and explainable AI.

My latest project is [AFRBench / AFRLib]({{ '/portfolio/afrbench/' | relative_url }}), an open-source benchmark and library for anti-facial-recognition research. AFRLib provides 26 method implementations, while AFRBench evaluates 24 methods under a common retrieval protocol. It brings together 45+ face-recognition models, 5,000 photos of 50 protectees, and 22.8 million distractor faces. See the [code and documentation](https://github.com/HKU-TASR/AFRBench).

I am the first author of [Protego](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_Protego_User-Centric_Pose-Invariant_Privacy_Protection_Against_Face_Recognition-Induced_Digital_Footprint_CVPR_2026_paper.html) and a coauthor of [TRivia](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_TRivia_Self-supervised_Fine-tuning_of_Vision-Language_Models_for_Table_Recognition_CVPR_2026_paper.html), both published at CVPR 2026. I also developed [FRBench](https://github.com/HKU-TASR/FRBench), a differentiable face-recognition model library used in privacy and robustness research.

Outside research, I advise the [HKU Herkules RoboMaster Team](https://github.com/HKUAstar) as a consultant, following my work on training and deploying detection models on edge devices. I also build software projects in FinTech and web development.

Welcome! I use this website to share my research, projects, and the occasional MPS debugging adventure. Feel free to [reach me by email](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
